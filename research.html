What we study

Research

We seek simple principles behind the strange and powerful ways quantum systems process information.

01

Quantum computation

What problems can quantum computers solve, and what resources make the advantage possible? We study algorithms, query complexity, and the structure of quantum complexity classes.

02

Quantum communication

We investigate the ultimate limits on sending classical and quantum information through noisy channels, including entanglement-assisted communication and quantum networks.

03

Error correction

Reliable quantum technologies require protection from noise. Our work develops codes and theoretical tools for fault-tolerant quantum computation.

04

Entanglement & many-body systems

Entanglement is both a resource and a lens on quantum matter. We explore its role in phases of matter, thermalization, and quantum statistical mechanics.

05

Foundations & cryptography

We use information-theoretic methods to illuminate the foundations of quantum theory and to design protocols with provable cryptographic security.
