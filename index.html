About the group

Curiosity, rigor, and collaboration.

We are a theoretical and computational research group, working primarily on a broad set of research topics that lie on the interface of quantum information and computation, quantum optics and many-body physics. Ranging from entanglement in multipartite systems to open dynamics of a spin-ensemble inside a quantum cavity and transient properties of photonic condensates, our research not only seeks to better understand the quantum domain but also to work on design and control of quantum devices and technologies of the future.
